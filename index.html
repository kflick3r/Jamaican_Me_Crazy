Our Very Relaxed Itinerary

Poolside Paradise

Our main agenda? Pool volleyball, swim-up bars, Caribbean sunshine, and absolutely no alarms. Grab a drink, claim a lounge chair, and enjoy island time!

Resort Fun

When we're ready for a break from the pool, we can check out wine tasting, reggae tie-dye, aqua reggae, beach bocce, or a relaxing resort activity.
Participation is entirely optional!

An Island Adventure or Two?

We might venture beyond the resort for one excursion! A catamaran cruise, sunset at Rick's Café, or a waterfall adventure could be on the menu. We can decide together!
Here are a few possibilities to explore:

  • Catamaran Cruise & Snorkeling

    Sail along the beautiful Jamaican coastline, enjoy some snorkeling, and maybe sip a tropical drink or two!

  • Rick's Cafe

    Watch the famous cliff divers, enjoy live music, and catch a gorgeous Caribbean sunset.

  • Mayfield Falls

    Explore lush tropical scenery, beautiful waterfalls, and natural swimming pools.

  • YS Falls

    Discover stunning waterfalls surrounded by tropical gardens, with opportunities for swimming and outdoor adventures.

Food, Drinks & Family Time

Delicious resort meals, tropical cocktails, and plenty of time together. No reservations on our calendars unless they're for dinner!
